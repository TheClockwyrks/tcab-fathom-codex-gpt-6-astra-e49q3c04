# Fathom

There is a light ahead. It might be dinner. It might be waiting for you. Fathom sends a tiny glowing forager into a flooded maze, where every mouthful of plankton makes the darkness retreat—and makes you easier to find.

Follow the motes through cold stone corridors. Your light reveals what lies nearby; sonar spills around corners and leaves paths in memory. In the Kindle dive, that memory is visible only inside the circle you carry. Stop feeding and the circle contracts, letting familiar ground disappear into the abyss again.

Three hunters listen to three different signals. The Lanternjaw disguises itself as a harmless amber jelly until your light draws its teeth. The eyeless Gloamfin answers sound with violet echoes and a relentless pursuit. The Flarefish burns a sudden window through the stone. Drop ink to break a light hunter's fix, or turn a tight corner to shake the hunter that hears you.

Eat the last mote and descend. Your score survives, the maze fills again, and another hunter joins the chase. Sonar is always coming back. Ink is always recharging. The difficult part is deciding when to announce that you are still alive.
